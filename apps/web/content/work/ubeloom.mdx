ubeloom sells lumpia ubi and fresh drinks in preorder batches. The site handles everything from the first order to pickup on campus, so a small team can run it without a spreadsheet of chat screenshots.

## The problem

Preorders over chat do not scale. Payments get lost in threads, proofs are screenshots of screenshots, and nobody knows which orders are ready for pickup.

## The storefront

Customers browse the menu, order while a batch is open, and choose pickup at Kampus Telkom, Bandung, or delivery at checkout. Each batch has a clear open and close time, so the kitchen knows exactly what to make.

## Behind the counter

The admin dashboard covers products, batches, vouchers, invoices, payment review, and fulfilment.

- Payment proofs are checked by their file bytes, not the browser's claimed type, then stored in a private S3 bucket.
- Orders move through payment and fulfilment states that the team can filter and update.
- A prize draw gives one entry per paid invoice for customers who opt in.

## Keeping the team in the loop

A separate Bun service runs a Telegram bot that notifies admins about pending payments and links them straight to the dashboard. Structured request logs make issues easy to trace.
