Mosque finances often live in a spreadsheet, a notebook, and someone's memory. ruangmasjid gives administrators and treasurers one workspace for every rupiah that comes in and goes out.

## The problem

Separate records make balances hard to trust. When a report is questioned, there is no easy way to show where a number came from, who changed it, or what supported it.

## Requirements first

The project started with a product requirements document, not code. It defines the users, the journeys, and testable success criteria, such as every report row reconciling to the closing balance: opening balance + income − expenses = closing balance.

## What it does

- Record income (`Uang Masuk`) and expenses (`Uang Keluar`) with categories, amounts in IDR, and optional proof.
- Import transactions from CSV and export reports.
- A dashboard with period totals, a cashflow chart, and recent transactions.
- A cash report (`Laporan Kas`) with a dated ledger and running balance.
- User management, mosque settings, and an opt-in public report for transparency.

## Trust by design

Changes to financial data are recorded in a change log that shows who did what and when, with filters and export. Roles separate who can record transactions from who can manage users and settings.
