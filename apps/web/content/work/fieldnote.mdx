Notes are easy to write and hard to find again. Fieldnote is a concept for a shared workspace where ideas stay connected to the work they came from. This is a dummy write-up while the real case study is on its way.

## The problem

Team knowledge lives in documents nobody opens twice. Decisions are made in a thread, summarised in a doc, and forgotten in a wiki. The context that made them make sense never travels with them.

## The approach

Fieldnote treats every note as a small unit that can be linked, quoted, and revisited. Notes carry where they came from, who they involve, and what they led to.

1. Capture quickly, without choosing a folder first.
2. Link a note to the work it informs.
3. Resurface it when that work comes up again.

> “Good notes are written for the person who forgot everything, including you.”

## What it could become

Real-time collaboration, a search that understands links as well as words, and a gentle weekly digest of what the team decided.

<Takeaway title="Where it could go">

Knowledge only helps when it shows up at the right moment. The interface should do the remembering so the team can do the thinking.

</Takeaway>
